# **[COUPLE] Flower** - _made by_ [_Spacy131_](https://modrinth.com/user/Spacy131)
Add a touch of **love** (and a bit of nature) to your Minecraft server!
**[COUPLE] Flower** is a multiplayer vanilla datapack that allows players to express their affection by giving flowers in real-time.


![[COUPLE] Flower - pack.png](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/5wXBBBnc/images/94d55e4e541b0c1aff9d896b2c2e82adf5521628.png)

---

## ✨ Features
- **💐Flower Gifting:** Simply hold a flower and sneak close to another player (within 2 blocks) to offer it to them. No commands needed!
![[COUPLE] Flower - Gifted](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/5wXBBBnc/images/3af56cce0488f4f4cdeb67e30d2fbbc543caadb8.png)

- **🎬 Sound & Visual Effects:** Trigger cherry leaves particle effects, chime sounds, and beautiful personalized moments when gifting flowers.
![[COUPLE] Flower - Particules](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/ea5e92d95a2bf64d9bc6da04c927115896193296.jpeg)

- **⏱️ Smart Cooldown & Mechanics:** Features a built-in 5-minute cooldown timer displayed via an interactive action bar progress bar!
Easily configurable directly inside 
`load.mcfunction`.
![[COUPLE] Flower - COOLDOWN](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/a971c37e9456da6f9151ba735987857eaee93b46.png)

### ⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNING

> If a player's username contains **more than 10 characters**, some on-screen title or subtitle displays (such as the *Flower* announcements) may slightly overlap or exceed the screen boundaries depending on the user's GUI scale settings. 

*This is a visual limitation due to Minecraft's title rendering engine and does not affect the performance or mechanics of the datapack.*
